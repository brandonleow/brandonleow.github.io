Mini B: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who uses Mini B

Mini B is a personal tool used only by its owner (github.com/brandonleow). Nobody else can sign in to it, so it does not collect anyone else's information.

2. What it gets from TikTok

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, TikTok gives Mini B:

  • basic profile information (an account ID, display name and profile picture), and
  • access keys that let Mini B send drafts to that account.

3. What it does with that

  • It uses the access keys for one thing: sending the owner's own photo slideshow drafts and captions to the owner's own TikTok account.
  • The access keys are kept in a file on the owner's own computer. They are not sent anywhere except to TikTok.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with other people or used for advertising.
  • It does not read, collect or store information about other TikTok users.

4. Pictures on this website

The slideshow pictures are placed on this website so that TikTok can fetch them. They are adverts made to be posted in public and contain no personal information.

5. Removing access

The owner can disconnect Mini B at any time in the TikTok app, under Settings > Security & permissions > Manage app permissions. Deleting the access file on the computer removes the stored keys.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

7. Contact

Questions: reach the owner through github.com/brandonleow.
