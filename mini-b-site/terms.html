Mini B: Terms of Service

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. What Mini B is

Mini B is a personal tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner (github.com/brandonleow). It is not offered to the public.

2. What it does with TikTok

With the owner's permission, given through TikTok's own login screen, Mini B sends photo slideshow drafts and a caption to the owner's own TikTok account. Each draft arrives in the TikTok app, where the owner reviews it and decides whether to post it. Mini B does not post anything by itself.

3. Rules it follows

  • It is only used with the owner's own TikTok account.
  • It follows TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms.
  • It does not like, comment, follow, message or collect information about other TikTok users.
  • The owner can disconnect it at any time in the TikTok app, under Settings > Security & permissions > Manage app permissions.

4. No warranty

Mini B is provided as it is, with no promise that it will always work.

If these terms change, the new version will be put on this page with a new date.
